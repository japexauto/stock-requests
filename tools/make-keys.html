Make phone alert keys

Do this once. The keys are made on this computer and aren't sent anywhere.

Public key

Goes in config.js as vapidPublicKey, and in Supabase secrets as VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY.

Private key

Goes ONLY in Supabase secrets as VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY. Don't put it in config.js or anywhere public.

If you make new keys later, everyone has to turn alerts off and on again.
